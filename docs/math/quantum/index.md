# Part 8: Quantum Computing Mathematics

Quantum computing operates in a mathematical universe that extends classical linear algebra to complex Hilbert spaces with tensor product structure. A quantum computer manipulates exponentially large state spaces through unitary transformations and extracts information through measurement — a fundamentally probabilistic process that has no classical analogue.

This part develops the complete mathematical framework: from complex vector spaces and Dirac notation through quantum gates, entanglement, and measurement theory, to the mathematical foundations of quantum algorithms and quantum machine learning.
