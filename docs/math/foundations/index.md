# Part 1: Foundations

Before vectors, matrices, or probability, we need the bedrock: the language and logic of mathematics itself. This part covers set theory, number systems, functions, and the notation you will encounter throughout every subsequent chapter and in ML research papers.

If you have a strong mathematical background, skim this part for notation conventions used in this text. If you're building from the ground up, work through each chapter carefully — these concepts reappear everywhere.

## Why This Matters for ML/AI

- **Set theory** underpins probability theory (events are sets), classification (decision boundaries partition sets), and formal language theory (context-free grammars)
- **Logic** is the foundation of Boolean classifiers, constraint satisfaction, and formal verification of neural networks
- **Number systems** determine data types in computation — integer quantization, floating-point precision, and complex amplitudes in quantum computing
- **Functions** are the mathematical objects that neural networks approximate — understanding injectivity, surjectivity, and composition is understanding what neural networks can and cannot represent
