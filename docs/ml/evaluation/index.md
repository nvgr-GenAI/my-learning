# Model Evaluation

**Measure and validate model performance.** Master metrics, cross-validation, and model selection techniques to build robust ML systems.

**Difficulty:** 🟢🟡 Beginner to Intermediate | **Time:** 1-2 weeks | **Prerequisites:** [ML Fundamentals](../fundamentals/index.md)

---

## 🎯 Why Model Evaluation Matters

**Model evaluation** determines how well your model will perform on unseen data. Without proper evaluation, you can't trust your model's predictions.

**Key Questions:**
- How accurate is my model?
- Which metric should I optimize?
- Is my model overfitting?
- How do I compare models?
- Will it generalize to new data?
